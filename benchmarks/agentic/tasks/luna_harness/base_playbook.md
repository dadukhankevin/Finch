# Shared Luna-harness methodology

This file contains campaign-level knowledge, not a champion implementation.

Keep the model, data, and compute boundary fixed. Improve what the Luna agent
does with the supplied evidence. Every experiment should name a mechanism, a
causal prediction, an activation condition, a fallback, and a falsifier.

Promising changes usually alter an actual decision: how evidence is organized,
how bilingual correspondences are induced, how alternatives are generated,
how semantic coverage is checked, how morphology and order are inferred, or
how conflicting corpus evidence is resolved. Hyperparameter changes are valid
only when tied to a concrete calibration hypothesis.

The exact top-ten examples are a strong starting point, not a ritual. The full
masked corpus is available to every candidate. A tool or research step should
earn its place by changing a downstream translation decision in a predictable
case. At a plateau, question an assumption or inject a genuinely different
idea. Natural crossover happens when a report genuinely extends, contrasts, or
combines cited shared findings. If an allocator also supplies exact parent
checkpoints for directed recombination, express one coherent interaction rather
than concatenating their instructions.

When a visible laboratory bundle is supplied, begin with the actual source,
candidate prediction, baseline prediction, and reference. Diagnose errors at
the sentence level before editing the harness. Confirm from commands and
`.runner` artifacts that an advertised tool really activated; a score cannot
validate a mechanism that never ran. Laboratory feedback is reusable training
data, while protected aggregate fitness remains the outer evolutionary test.

A language-agnostic anomaly critic is a worthwhile research family when it
surfaces corpus-backed evidence rather than forcing a rewrite. Useful warnings
may concern missing or unsupported content, unusual target-side form, abnormal
length, names and quantities, polarity or role inconsistency, and disagreement
among independent retrieved analogies. Report confidence and counterevidence,
let Luna decide, and abstain when the supplied corpus cannot resolve the issue.
